Our paper **PW-OPSD** has been accepted to **NeurIPS 2026** as a poster! We show that teacher-token reliability in on-policy self-distillation for reasoning is position-structured — within-sequence position predicts reliability far better than local uncertainty (AUROC 0.83 vs. 0.57) — and propose Position-Weighted On-Policy Self-Distillation (PW-OPSD), which up-weights reliable later tokens at no extra teacher cost. Check out our [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.21606) and [code](https://github.com/SaFo-Lab/PW-OPSD).
