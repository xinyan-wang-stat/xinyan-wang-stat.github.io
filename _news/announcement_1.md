**ReasoningBomb** is now on [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.00154). We show that large reasoning models are vulnerable to prompt-induced denial-of-service attacks — seemingly innocent prompts can trigger pathologically long reasoning chains, achieving 286.7x token amplification and reducing benign user throughput by 49.8%. Check out our [website](https://reasoningbomb.github.io) and [code](https://github.com/SaFo-Lab/ReasoningBomb).
