Our new paper **Appreciation Teaches Unauthorized Execution (Evolve-IPI)** is now on [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.39122). We show that self-evolution can weaken personal agents' robustness to indirect prompt injection. Check out our [code](https://github.com/SaFo-Lab/Evolve-IPI) and [dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Evolve-IPI/Evolve-IPI).
