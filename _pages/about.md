I am **Xinyan Wang**, a fourth year PhD student in Statistics at the University of Wisconsin–Madison, advised by Professors [Jun Shao](https://pages.stat.wisc.edu/~shao/) and [Xiao Luo](https://luoxiao12.github.io/), and working with Professor [Chaowei Xiao](https://xiaocw11.github.io/) at Johns Hopkins University. I received my BS in Statistics from East China Normal University in 2022 and MS in Statistics from UW–Madison in 2023. I am also pursuing a MS in Computer Science at UW–Madison.

I work on **LLM post-training**, including reinforcement learning (RL) and on-policy distillation (OPD), and on **LLM agents**. My goal is to make reasoning models and agents efficient, reliable, and safe in practice through post-training, inference-time control, and red-teaming. I am currently working on **multi-agent systems**, with a focus on **communication between agents**. My current research focuses on:

- **Post-Training and Self-Evolution:** Studying how models and agents learn through agentic reinforcement learning, user feedback, and on-policy distillation. Recent work includes [Evolve-IPI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.39122) and [PW-OPSD](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.21606).
- **Safety and Robustness:** Understanding and red-teaming vulnerabilities in self-evolving agents, decision models, and reasoning models. Recent work includes [Evolve-IPI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.39122), [FlipJev](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.38895), and [ReasoningBomb](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.00154).
- **Efficient Reasoning:** Understanding and controlling overthinking to reduce inference cost while preserving accuracy. Recent work includes [ROM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.22016).
